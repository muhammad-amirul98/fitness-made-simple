Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 August 2026.

Fitness Made Simple SG ("I", "me") is a solo personal training business run by Muhammad Amirul in Singapore. This page explains what personal data I collect through this website and coaching relationship, why, and how it's handled — in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What I collect

When you use the contact or intake forms on this site, I collect what you choose to enter, which may include:

  • Name, email, and phone number
  • Age and gender
  • Training goals, experience level, available equipment, and preferred schedule
  • Injuries, medical conditions, or physical limitations you disclose ("health-adjacent" data)
  • Anything else you write in a free-text field

If you become a client, I also collect ongoing check-in data — workout logs, progress photos, and messages — that you send me directly as part of coaching.

The site also uses Google Analytics (GA4), which automatically collects anonymised usage data like pages visited, device type, and approximate location. This isn't tied to your name unless you separately submit a form.

Why I collect it

Solely to run the coaching relationship: to reply to your inquiry, design and adjust a program safely around your goals and any physical limitations, schedule calls, and communicate with you. Analytics data is used only to understand which pages are useful and where to improve the site.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored in a private Google Sheet, accessible only to me, via a Google Apps Script connected to this site. Ongoing coaching communication happens over email and WhatsApp. None of this data is stored on a public-facing database or server.

Who else sees it

I don't sell or rent your data to anyone. It passes through the infrastructure providers that make the service work — Google (Sheets, Apps Script, Analytics), WhatsApp/Meta for messaging, and Netlify for hosting this site — each bound by their own privacy terms. I don't share your data with any other third party.

How long I keep it

For as long as we have an active coaching relationship, plus a reasonable period after (to handle any follow-up questions). If you were never a client and just submitted an inquiry, I'll delete it if you ask, or periodically clear out old, inactive leads.

Your rights

Under the PDPA, you can ask to see what data I hold on you, correct it, withdraw consent for me to use it, or have it deleted. To do any of this, just email me — see below.

Security

Access to the Google Sheet and email account holding your data is restricted to me alone, protected by standard account security (2-factor authentication). As a solo operator I don't have a dedicated IT/security team, but I take reasonable, standard precautions with the tools above.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you want to exercise any of the rights above, email fitnessmadesimplesg@gmail.com.
