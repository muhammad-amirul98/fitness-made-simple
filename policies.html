Package & Cancellation Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026. Applies to External Training and Studio Training session packages. Online Coaching isn't open yet — its billing and cancellation terms will be published when it launches.

Cancelling or rescheduling a session

Give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel a session, and it won't count against your package. Less than 24 hours' notice, or a no-show, uses one session. If something genuinely unusual comes up — a sudden emergency — message me directly; exceptions are considered case by case.

If I need to cancel

If I have to cancel or reschedule a session, it's never deducted from your package. I'll do my best to find a replacement slot promptly. If I'm ever unable to continue coaching you, I'll refund the value of any sessions you haven't used yet, using the method below.

Package validity

Packages are valid for 12 months from your first session. Book that first session within 30 days of purchase to start the clock. If something significant comes up — illness, an extended trip — message me before your package expires and I'll pause it for up to 30 days, extending the expiry date to match.

Refunds

If you want to stop partway through a package, I'll refund your unused sessions at the package's effective average price — what you paid, divided by the total number of sessions in the package, including any free bonus sessions. For example, a 20-session External Training package costs $1,520 (19 paid sessions plus 1 bonus session), which works out to $76/session. If you've used 5 sessions, the remaining 15 are worth $1,140.

Referral offer

If a friend refers you, you still choose and pay for a package as normal — the referral offer means your first session in that package is on the house. It has no cash value, can't be combined with other discounts, and applies once per new client.

Questions

Anything here unclear, or want to check something before you commit to a package? Email fitnessmadesimplesg@gmail.com or message on WhatsApp — happy to answer before you pay for anything.
