Liability Waiver & Informed Consent

Last updated 14 August 2026. Read this before starting training with Fitness Made Simple SG.

Assumption of risk

Exercise carries an inherent risk of injury — including but not limited to muscle strain, joint injury, and, in rare cases, more serious harm — even when a program is well-designed and followed correctly. By training with Fitness Made Simple SG, you acknowledge this risk and voluntarily choose to participate.

Your responsibility to disclose

Programs are built around what you tell me. You're responsible for accurately disclosing any injuries, medical conditions, physical limitations, or changes to your health as they happen — before we start, and at any point during coaching. If you're unsure whether you're medically fit to begin an exercise program, check with a doctor first.

Not medical advice

I'm not a licensed physician, physiotherapist, or dietitian, and nothing provided as part of coaching — programs, check-in feedback, or general guidance — is medical advice or a substitute for one. Where something looks like it needs professional medical attention, I'll say so and point you to a qualified professional rather than trying to handle it myself.

Nature of online coaching

Coaching is delivered remotely — through a program, video demos, check-ins, and periodic live calls, not in-person supervision at every session. You're responsible for performing exercises as demonstrated, at your own pace, in a safe environment, and for stopping if something feels wrong. Send a form-check video or ask before continuing if you're ever unsure about an exercise.

Voluntary participation

You can pause, modify, or stop any exercise, session, or the coaching relationship as a whole at any time, for any reason.

Release of liability

To the extent permitted by Singapore law, by training with Fitness Made Simple SG you release Muhammad Amirul / Fitness Made Simple SG from liability for injury or loss arising from your participation, except where caused by gross negligence or wilful misconduct.
