Why a coach

An app can log your workout. It can't tell you why you stopped.

Fitness apps are fine at generating a plan. What they can't do is notice you've missed three check-ins in a row, ask what's actually going on, and adjust around real life — a rough week at work, an injury, motivation that's dropped off. That's the part that actually keeps people training long-term, and it's the part software can't do.

  • One person who knows your history — not a new algorithm run every week
  • A program that adjusts to your actual week, not a static plan you're stuck following
  • Someone accountable for asking how it actually went, not just logging that it happened
Is this you

Built for people who want training that finally sticks

  • You're busy — work, travel, life — and need a plan that flexes around your week, not one you have to flex your life around
  • You've started and stopped before, and want one person tracking the whole picture instead of another app to check
  • You want a program built around your actual schedule and equipment, not a generic template
  • You want structure and a second set of eyes — not someone chasing you down when life gets busy
