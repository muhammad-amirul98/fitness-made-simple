Getting Started with Fitness Made Simple SG

Read this once after you sign up — it's your reference for how coaching actually runs week to week.

Welcome

You've done the intro call and the intake form — here's exactly what happens next, and how the day-to-day of coaching works. Keep this page bookmarked; you shouldn't need to guess how anything works after reading it once.

Booking your sessions (External Training)

If you're doing External Training rather than Online Coaching, book each session directly here: https://cal.com/fitnessmadesimplesg/1-hour-pt-session. The rest of this page (program sheet, weekly check-ins, biweekly calls) is written for Online Coaching — feel free to stop here.

Between now and your first check-in

  • I'll build your first program from your intake form and send it to you within 2 business days.
  • You'll get it as a link to your own program sheet — see below.
  • Your first weekly check-in lands on Tuesday, whether or not you've completed a full week yet — it's fine to check in on partial progress.

Your program

Your program lives in a Google Sheet — exercises, sets, reps, load, and a notes column for how each session actually felt. It's not a fixed block that repeats for 12 weeks unchanged; I adjust it around your check-ins and how you're progressing.

Unsure about an exercise? Send a form-check video over WhatsApp any time — don't wait for check-in day to ask.

Weekly check-ins

Every Tuesday, I'll message you on WhatsApp with the same five quick questions:

  • Adherence — how many planned sessions did you complete?
  • Energy & sleep, roughly, on a 1-5
  • Any pain, soreness, or niggles?
  • Progress photo or weight — optional, your call
  • Wins or blockers this week

Reply whenever works for you. I'll get back to you within 24 hours. If you miss a week, there's no penalty — just pick back up on the next one.

Your live call, every two weeks

Every two weeks, we'll get on a call to go deeper than a WhatsApp check-in can. Book or reschedule your slot here: https://cal.com/fitnessmadesimplesg/check-in. Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to move it.

What I need from you

  • Honest logging. A skipped session logged honestly is more useful to your program than a fudged "yes."
  • Flag pain or anything that feels off immediately — don't sit on it until check-in day.
  • Message any time for non-urgent questions. WhatsApp is always open, and the 24-hour reply window applies whenever you reach out, not just on check-in day.

If you need to pause or stop

Life happens — travel, injury, a rough patch at work. Message me any time and we'll pause your coaching with no charge while paused. Pick back up whenever you're ready; your program will be waiting, adjusted if anything's changed. No fixed notice period, no penalty for pausing.

Quick answers

  • Something feels off or painful mid-week. Message me immediately on WhatsApp — don't wait for check-in day.
  • Travel or life got in the way. Tell me as soon as you know. We'll adjust the program around it rather than you going quiet and starting over later.
  • You want to change something about your program. Check-ins are the natural place for this, but message any time if it can't wait a week.

Questions
