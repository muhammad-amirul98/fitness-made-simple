Making the switch: from software engineer to personal trainer

For the last two years I've made my living writing code. For over a decade before that — and the whole time, really — I've been in the gym. First for myself, then coaching friends who wanted to start but didn't know where to begin. This year I'm making it official: I'm working toward my NCSF personal training certification, expected this October, and building Fitness Made Simple SG around it.

Why now

Honestly, it's less a switch and more catching up to what I was already doing. "Can you help me get started" has been the most common favor friends have asked me for years — more than anything to do with my actual job. I kept saying yes because I liked it and I was good at it. At some point doing it for free on the side stopped making sense when I could just do it properly.

What to expect if you train with me

Beginner-friendly, no judgment — most of the people I've coached had never set foot in a gym before. Personalized programming, not a template — your starting point, schedule, and goals shape the plan, not the other way round. And sessions built for people with full calendars: efficient, clear, no wasted time.

Where things stand right now

I'm completing my NCSF certification, expected October 2026, and taking on a limited number of early clients in the East, CBD, and online across Singapore while I do. If this sounds like what you're looking for, get in touch — I'd genuinely like to hear where you're starting from.
