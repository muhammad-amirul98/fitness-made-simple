Niche

I spent over a decade lifting, and just as long coaching friends through the basics — form, programming, and the confidence to actually walk into a gym without feeling out of place.

Before this, I was a software engineer. That background shows up in how I coach: I like systems that work, numbers that mean something, and programs that don't waste your time. No gimmicks, no guesswork — just a structured plan built around your schedule and your goals.

I'm currently completing my NCSF Certified Personal Trainer certification, expected October 2026, to bring that experience into full-time coaching.

My own training as a teenager felt confusing and intimidating — machines with no instructions, programs copied off the internet with no context. I coach the way I wish someone had coached me: plainly, patiently, and with a plan I could actually understand.

PHILOSOPHY

Three things I believe about training.

A

Consistency beats intensity

A sustainable plan you'll actually follow beats a perfect one you'll quit in three weeks.

B

Understanding builds confidence

If you know why you're doing something, you'll keep doing it — with or without me in the room.

C

Data over guesswork

Progress should be visible, not a feeling. What gets tracked gets improved.
